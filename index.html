About

Filmmaker, colorist and electrical engineering student.

I’m William, a filmmaker and colorist based in Charlotte, NC. I focus on narrative storytelling, emotional lighting, and rich, cinematic color.

With a background in electrical engineering, I love the technical side of cameras, color pipelines, and workflows just as much as the creative side. Whether it’s a short film, a commercial, or a documentary interview, my goal is to make images that feel honest and intentional.
